# Creative Brief — Study/Work Vlog

## Story identity
A normal working person's postgraduate-exam preparation life: early wake-up, 30-minute workout, English study, workday fragments used for review, incline walking after work, and 312 study at night.

The emotional center is not “perfect discipline.” It is: tired sometimes, distracted sometimes, still moving forward a little.

## Editing language
- Prefer details and actions over explanatory talking-head footage.
- Establish each block with one wider shot, then 2–4 detail shots.
- Typical selected shot length: 1.5–4 seconds; allow 5–8 seconds when ambience/emotion benefits.
- Use hard cuts, motivated cuts, J/L cuts, and natural sound bridges.
- Leave occasional subtitle-free breathing room.
- Avoid excessive speed ramps, zoom transitions, stickers, motivational slogans, and constant on-screen text.

## Episode 01 structure
1. 05:30 wake-up / morning atmosphere
2. 30-minute workout
3. English study
4. 07:30 work transition
5. workday + fragmented review time
6. after-work incline walking
7. evening 312 study
8. quiet closing beat rather than a forced inspirational ending

## Subtitle voice
Write as if messaging a friend, not reporting a schedule.

Good examples:
- 今天也是不太想起床的一天。 / Another morning when I really don't want to get up.
- 五点半的脑子，还没完全开机。 / My brain is definitely not online at 5:30.
- 上班之后，学习只能见缝插针。 / Once work starts, studying happens in the little gaps.
- 下班了，但今天还没结束。 / Work's over. The day isn't.

Avoid:
- 05:30 起床 / Wake up at 05:30
- 坚持就是胜利 / Persistence is victory
- 自律的人生才自由 / Discipline equals freedom

## Bilingual subtitle rules
- Chinese carries personality and meaning.
- English should sound natural rather than mirror Chinese word-for-word.
- Keep both lines short enough to read comfortably.
- Do not subtitle every shot.
- Never invent thoughts or emotions the creator did not express.

## Sound
Use room tone, alarm/bed movement, water, pages, keyboard, footsteps, gym machine ambience, and other useful diegetic sound. Music should support rather than cover daily-life texture.

## Reference principle
The creator likes the calm, intimate everyday-vlog feeling associated with Bemyself. Use that only as high-level inspiration for pacing and intimacy; do not copy another creator's exact shots, graphics, wording, or distinctive edit sequence.
